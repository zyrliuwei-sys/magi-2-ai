## What the official preview shows

### Character performance

Watch how expression, eye movement, and body language develop together. Check what happens when a character looks away or turns into profile. A consistent silhouette and wardrobe make the performance easier to follow across changing angles.

### Camera movement

Read the first frame before following the camera. Identify the subject and the background lines that establish depth. Then note when the camera starts moving and whether it follows the subject, reveals something new, or ends on a detail.

### Dialogue and sound

Listen for a spoken line while watching the mouth and the listener’s reaction. A pause, a glance, or a change in room tone can mark the end of one beat before the next starts. Watch once with sound and once with captions or a transcript where available.

## How MAGI-2 Preview differs from MAGI-1

MAGI-1 generates video in sequential chunks for streaming and continuation. The August 2026 MAGI-2 Preview is a research release that scales a single model for both picture and sound. It uses a mixture-of-experts architecture to route each part of the input through a subset of the model.

Sand.ai’s November flagship announcement describes a later MAGI-2 model with multimodal reference inputs and more timeline control. Check which release or hosted model a demo uses before comparing it with the research preview.

## How to try MAGI-2

**Sand.ai.** Open the research preview or full announcement in Sources to study the original examples. For a hosted workflow, follow the official partner link on Sand.ai’s homepage and check the model available there. To run the research preview yourself, use Sand.ai’s Preview repository and its instructions for acquiring the weights and running the sample batch.

**Higgsfield.** Open the model showcase in Sources and choose **Try MAGI-2**. Select the model and sign in if prompted. Paste a shot brief, add supported references when needed, and review the settings and cost before you render.
