# magi-2 ai visual identity

magi-2 ai is an independent short-video studio for product creators and storytellers. Its visual reference is a screening room, with warm projected light, generous margins, and clear camera controls.

## Palette

| Role            | Color                   | Use                                        |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Obsidian        | `oklch(0.168 0.012 58)` | Public page background and image frames    |
| Warm charcoal   | `oklch(0.205 0.014 61)` | Studio and card surfaces                   |
| Smoke           | `oklch(0.244 0.015 63)` | Selected controls and layered surfaces     |
| Ivory           | `oklch(0.959 0.017 81)` | Headings and primary text                  |
| Champagne brass | `oklch(0.846 0.085 79)` | Primary actions, links, and focus outlines |
| Warm gray       | `oklch(0.759 0.028 77)` | Secondary text and captions                |

The semantic palette lives in `src/styles/globals.css`. The public pages share the dark screening-room palette. Account surfaces also have a warm light palette for the application’s light appearance setting.

## Type and mark

- **Instrument Serif** sets page titles and editorial headings.
- **Geist** sets body copy, navigation, and controls.
- **System monospace** sets duration, aspect ratio, and character counts.
- The mark uses a raised brass **M inside four viewfinder corners** on an obsidian base. Use `/imgs/magi/logo-3d.png` for the site logo and favicon.

## Layout and photography

The live studio pairs a compact form with a wide preview. Galleries give the main still a larger frame. Guides place a contents rail beside a readable text column. Use fine borders to mark controls and reading sections and use brass for actions and focus.

The amber glass still was created with ImageGen using a square perfume bottle, black stone, and a narrow band of warm studio light. It serves as a starting image and the basis of the social sharing image. Official MAGI-2 footage carries its source attribution in the examples library.
